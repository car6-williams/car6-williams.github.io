I will be making a fanny pack for the club. 

Pink and Blue LED lights will constantly flash, but when the party starts and the room turns dark a flashing RGB light will shine like a strobing lazer. 

Don’t worry if you need a cigarette. If you step outside and want the party to continue just flick a switch and music will start playing. This way you can dance while smoking.
