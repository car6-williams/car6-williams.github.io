In this assignment we had to made a button that slected a random color. When the button was pressed a r,g, and b value was decided genertating a random color. When the button was let go it would start to rotate through colors until pressed.

A tip for the future would be to put a paper on top of the light. It is so bright and blinding especially when flashing so rapidly.
